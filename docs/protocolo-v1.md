Fungi Mati
Mushroom Cultivation Guide
Guia de Cultivo de Hongos

What is Fungi Mati? / Que es Fungi Mati?
An accessible biotechnology system that converts locally available organic waste into high-protein edible mushrooms. No electricity, gas, land, or prior experience required. Anyone with access to organic residues can implement it.
Un sistema de biotecnologia accesible que convierte residuos organicos locales en hongos comestibles de alta proteina. No requiere electricidad, gas, tierra, ni experiencia previa.

Step 3 / Paso 3: Choose your substrate / Elige tu sustrato
The substrate is the material in which the mushrooms will grow. Use whatever is available locally. / El sustrato es el material en el que creceran los hongos. Usa lo que tengas disponible localmente.
Fibrous or large-structure substrates / Sustratos fibrosos o de estructura grande:
Wheat straw, oat straw, rice straw, wood shavings, cardboard, coconut fiber, agave fiber, corn stalks. These use Protocol A. / Paja de trigo, paja de avena, paja de arroz, viruta de madera, carton, fibra de coco, fibra de agave, rastrojo de maiz. Estos van al Protocolo A.
Powdered or pelleted substrates / Sustratos pulverizados o en pellets:
Wood pellets, fine sawdust, coffee grounds. These use Protocol B. / Pellets de madera, asserrin fino, bagazo de cafe. Estos van al Protocolo B.

Step 4 / Paso 4: Substrate preparation / Preparacion del sustrato
Protocol A: Fibrous substrates / Protocolo A: Sustratos fibrosos
For straw, wood shavings, cardboard, coconut fiber, agave fiber, corn stalks: / Para paja, viruta, carton, fibra de coco, fibra de agave, rastrojo de maiz:

1. For every 5 liters of water, dissolve 1 tablespoon of hydrated lime (approx. 10-15 grams). / Por cada 5 litros de agua, disuelve 1 cucharada de cal hidratada (aprox. 10-15 gramos).
2. Bring the lime water to a boil. / Lleva el agua con cal a ebullicion.
3. Submerge the substrate in the boiling solution and keep on medium heat for 1 hour. / Sumerge el sustrato en la solucion hirviendo y mantente a fuego medio durante 1 hora.
4. Remove from heat and allow to cool completely to room temperature. / Retira del fuego y deja enfriar completamente.
5. Drain the substrate until it no longer drips but remains moist to the touch, like a well-wrung wet cloth. / Escurre el sustrato hasta que ya no gotee pero siga humedo al tacto, como un trapo mojado bien escurrido.

Protocol B: Pelleted or powdered substrates / Protocolo B: Sustratos pulverizados o en pellets
For wood pellets, sawdust, coffee grounds: / Para pellets de madera, asserrin, bagazo de cafe:

6. Place the dry substrate directly inside your bag or container. / Coloca el sustrato seco directamente dentro de tu bolsa o recipiente.
7. For every 5 liters of water, dissolve 1 tablespoon of hydrated lime and bring to a boil. / Por cada 5 litros de agua, disuelve 1 cucharada de cal hidratada y lleva a ebullicion.
8. Pour the hot lime water over the substrate inside the bag. The amount is 60% of the dry weight of the substrate. Example: 1 kg dry substrate gets 600 ml hot lime solution. / Vierte el agua con cal caliente sobre el sustrato. La cantidad es el 60% del peso seco del sustrato. Ejemplo: 1 kg de sustrato seco, 600 ml de solucion.
9. The substrate will be pasteurized and hydrated at the same time. / El sustrato se pasteurizara e hidratara al mismo tiempo.
10. Allow to cool completely before inoculating. / Deja enfriar completamente antes de inocular.

Step 5 / Paso 5: Inoculation / Inoculacion

11. Wash your hands thoroughly with soap. / Lava bien tus manos con jabon.
12. If your bag or container was previously used, sanitize it with 70% alcohol as a precaution. / Si tu bolsa o recipiente fue usado antes, sanitizalo con alcohol al 70%.
13. Weigh your drained substrate and calculate spawn: use 10% of the weight. Without a scale, estimate: a generous handful of spawn per kilogram of substrate. The error margin is wide: as little as 3-5% can work, just more slowly. / Pesa tu sustrato escurrido y calcula el micelio: usa el 10% del peso. Sin bascula, estima a ojo: un punado generoso por kilogramo. Desde un 3-5% funciona aunque mas lento.
14. Distribute spawn evenly throughout the substrate by mixing with your hands or shaking the bag vigorously. / Distribuye el micelio uniformemente mezclando con las manos o sacudiendo la bolsa con fuerza.
15. Close the bag with a simple loose knot. It should allow some gas exchange without letting in moisture or insects. Any plastic bag works: grocery bag, trash bag, any type. / Cierra la bolsa con un nudo simple, no muy apretado. Cualquier bolsa de plastico funciona.

Step 6 / Paso 6: Incubation / Incubacion
Place the bag in a dark or dim, cool location with no air drafts. A closet, a shoebox, or under a bed work perfectly. / Coloca la bolsa en un lugar oscuro o con penumbra, fresco y sin corrientes de aire. Un closet, una caja de zapatos o debajo de una cama funcionan perfectamente.
Ideal temperature: 24-28 degrees Celsius. / Temperatura ideal: 24-28 grados Celsius.
During the first 7 days, observe the bag without opening it. A radial white or beige growth spreading from the spawn points is the sign that everything is working well, and almost guarantees success by day 21. / Durante los primeros 7 dias observa la bolsa sin abrirla. Un crecimiento radial blanco partiendo del micelio es senal de exito casi asegurado al dia 21.
Full incubation takes approximately 21 days. / La incubacion completa toma aproximadamente 21 dias.

Warning signs during incubation / Senales de alerta durante la incubacion
Green or black patches / Manchas verdes o negras:
Contamination by competing fungi. Discard that bag immediately. Do not open it near your other bags. / Contaminacion por hongos competidores. Desecha esa bolsa inmediatamente. No la abras cerca de las demas.
Strong fermented or sour smell / Olor fuerte a fermentado o agrio:
Bacterial contamination, likely caused by excess moisture in the substrate at the time of inoculation. / Contaminacion bacteriana, probablemente por exceso de humedad en el sustrato al momento de inocular.
Very limited or no growth, no patches, no bad smell / Micelio con crecimiento muy limitado o nulo:
The substrate may be too dry, the spawn may be old, or the spawn was exposed to direct sunlight before inoculation. / El sustrato puede estar muy seco, el micelio era viejo, o fue expuesto a luz solar directa antes de inocular.
Important: if contamination appears within the first 8 days, the spawn likely arrived contaminated from the source. File a buyer protection claim on Mercado Libre before discarding the protocol. / Importante: si la contaminacion aparece en los primeros 8 dias, el micelio probablemente llego contaminado desde origen. Aplica la garantia del vendedor en Mercado Libre.

Step 7 / Paso 7: Fruiting induction / Induccion de fructificacion
When the mycelium has fully colonized the bag (approximately day 21): / Cuando el micelio haya colonizado completamente la bolsa (aproximadamente dia 21):

16. Make one or several cuts in the bag with scissors or a knife. / Haz uno o varios cortes en la bolsa con tijeras o cuchillo.
17. Begin misting with a spray bottle 2-3 times per day. Maintain this consistently. / Comienza a regar con aspersor 2-3 veces al dia. Mantener ese riego de manera constante.
18. Place the bag in a spot with indirect light. Avoid direct sunlight and air drafts: both can cause the mushroom to abort its fruit. / Coloca la bolsa en un lugar con luz indirecta. Evita luz solar directa y corrientes de aire.
19. Fruiting occurs between 7 and 14 days after induction. / La fructificacion ocurre entre 7 y 14 dias despues de la induccion.

Step 8 / Paso 8: Harvest / Cosecha
Harvest when the mushroom cap has its edges curled downward, before it opens completely. At this point texture and flavor are optimal. / Cosecha cuando el sombrero tenga los bordes enrollados hacia abajo, antes de que se abran completamente.
Cut from the base with a clean knife or scissors. / Corta desde la base con cuchillo o tijera limpia.
A single bag can produce 2 to 3 consecutive harvests. Each flush occurs approximately every 2 weeks. Keep the substrate moist between harvests with the spray bottle. / Una sola bolsa puede producir 2 a 3 cosechas. Cada una ocurre aproximadamente cada 2 semanas. Mantener la humedad entre cosechas.

What to do with spent substrate / Que hago con el sustrato agotado?
When the bag no longer produces, the spent substrate is excellent as compost for plants or as a feed supplement for pigs, chickens, or cattle. / Cuando la bolsa ya no produce, el sustrato agotado es excelente como composta para plantas o como alimento para cerdos, gallinas o ganado.

Frequently Asked Questions / Preguntas frecuentes
What kind of bag should I use? / Que bolsa uso?
Any plastic bag works: grocery bag, trash bag, any type. No specific requirements. / Cualquier bolsa de plastico funciona: del mandado, de basura, cualquier tipo. No hay especificaciones tecnicas requeridas.
Where can I buy spawn in Mexico? / Donde compro el micelio en Mexico?
Mercado Libre is the most reliable option with buyer protection. Search for 'micelio Pleurotus' and choose sellers with recent positive reviews. / Mercado Libre es la opcion mas confiable. Busca micelio Pleurotus y elige vendedores con resenas positivas recientes.
How long does stored spawn last? / Cuanto tiempo dura el micelio almacenado?
Approximately 6 months at room temperature, as long as it is not contaminated or exposed to direct light. / Aproximadamente 6 meses a temperatura ambiente, siempre que no se contamine ni se exponga a luz directa.
How much does it cost to start? / Cuanto cuesta empezar?
Approximately $10 USD per cultivation unit, including spawn. The substrate can be sourced for free as local waste. / Aproximadamente 10 USD por unidad de cultivo, incluyendo micelio. El sustrato puede conseguirse gratuitamente como residuo local.
Do I need special equipment? / Necesito equipo especial?
No. Just a pot, water, hydrated lime, a spray bottle, and a plastic bag you already have at home. / No. Solo una olla, agua, cal hidratada, un aspersor y la bolsa de plastico que ya tienes en casa.
Can I use any type of organic waste? / Puedo usar cualquier tipo de residuo organico?
Pleurotus mushrooms grow on any material rich in lignin or cellulose: wheat or oat or rice straw, wood shavings, wood pellets, coconut fiber, agave fiber, cardboard, coffee grounds, corn stalks. Use whatever is available in your region. / Los hongos Pleurotus crecen en cualquier material rico en lignina o celulosa. Usa lo que tengas disponible en tu region.
My mycelium is not growing after 21 days. What do I do? / Mi micelio no crece despues de 21 dias, que hago?
If there is no visible growth and no signs of contamination, check three possible causes: the substrate is too dry, the spawn was old, or the spawn was exposed to direct light before or during incubation. If there are colored patches or bad smells, it is contamination. / Si no hay crecimiento visible y no hay senales de contaminacion, revisa: sustrato muy seco, micelio viejo, o micelio expuesto a luz directa.
How many times can one bag produce? / Cuantas veces puede producir una bolsa?
Between 2 and 3 consecutive harvests, each occurring approximately every 2 weeks. Keep moisture levels up between harvests with a spray bottle. / Entre 2 y 3 cosechas consecutivas, cada una aproximadamente cada 2 semanas. Mantener la humedad entre cosechas con el aspersor.
